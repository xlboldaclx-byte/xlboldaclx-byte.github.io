Circle Bar V Workshop / Private desktop application

Your channel.
Your command.

Empire Command Bridge helps the Workshop’s owner review channel performance, prepare reports and plan the next piece of content.

The application runs on the owner’s computer. Its assistant, Revan, can retrieve YouTube channel information and non-monetary analytics after the owner grants permission through Google.

The YouTube connection

A clear view of
channel performance.

01

Identify the intended channel

Read the channel’s ID, title and handle so the owner can confirm which channel is connected.

02

Read aggregate analytics

Retrieve views, estimated watch minutes and subscribers gained for completed reporting periods. Missing permission is reported as unavailable.

03

Review and plan

Use the results in private reports and, when requested, AI-assisted analysis. See the privacy policy for storage, external processing and owner controls.

Permission, in plain language

Read access. Owner control.

The current connector requests youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. These support channel information and non-monetary analytics. The connector does not request video upload, editing, deletion or monetary-report permissions.

The owner signs in with Google in a browser, reviews permissions and confirms the channel in the local application. Access can be revoked in Google Account connections.
